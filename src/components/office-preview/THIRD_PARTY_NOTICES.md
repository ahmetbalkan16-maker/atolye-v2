## `yapayzekaofisi/kendi-ofisini-kur`

The human character design in `AtolyeOffice3DScene.tsx` adapts the procedural Three.js character approach in [`baslangic-ofisi/public/ofis3d.js`](https://github.com/yapayzekaofisi/kendi-ofisini-kur/blob/main/baslangic-ofisi/public/ofis3d.js): articulated human proportions, capsule limbs, varied skin/hair/clothing, role-specific details, and seated/standing poses. The office preview keeps its own code and implementation; it does not bundle the repository's source file or a character image asset.
