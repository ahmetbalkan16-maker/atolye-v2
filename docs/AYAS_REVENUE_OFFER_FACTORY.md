# AYAS offer / product factory — Stage16.3A

`buildAyasRevenueOffer(input, trustedNow)` is a pure, deterministic advisory function. It turns one Stage16.3 free-first validation input plus normalized fulfillment receipts into a platform-independent local offer draft, or refuses. Every result carries `authority: NONE`, `sellableAutonomously: false`, `externalWrite: false`, `grantsSpendAuthority: false` and `evidenceVerification: NORMALIZED_INPUT_NOT_EXTERNAL_CERTIFICATION`. No registry, filesystem, network, credential, ledger store or executor is imported; `AyasRevenueDigest.ts` adds only `node:crypto` and the existing canonical JSON serializer.

An offer names the canonical 16.3A fields as bounded codes, never prose: value proposition and target customer segment, deliverables (code, deliverable class, capability key, units 1–100), revision and support bounds (at most 10 revisions, 1,440 support minutes, 365 support days), a delivery window (at most 30 days) and platform mappings. A mapping's operation is code-owned and local only: Etsy, Fiverr and Lemon Squeezy `LISTING_DRAFT`, Upwork `PROPOSAL_DRAFT`, Udemy `COURSE_DRAFT`. The price scenario is copied from the validated hypothetical scenario and stays `ASSUMED`; it cannot be admitted as a Stage16.2 ledger fact.

Three outcomes exist. `BLOCKED`: invalid or directive-bearing input, Stage16.3 `BLOCKED`/`DO_NOT_PURSUE`, an offer outside the validated opportunity (other opportunity, other value proposition, a deliverable capability the opportunity does not list, a future creation time), any paid/unknown-cost, failed-quality or blocked-rights receipt, or a delivery promise beyond measured capacity. `DRAFT_INCOMPLETE`: unknown price or cost, validation weaker than `PILOT_CANDIDATE`/`OWNER_REVIEW_REQUIRED`, rights not `CLEAR`, deliverable not `PROVEN`, no covering current measured sample, or unknown/stale capacity. `OWNER_REVIEW_READY`: a local draft for the owner; the reason code says the owner and each target platform must still revalidate it. Publishing, proposing, messaging and every financial action keep their separate owner and spend gates.

Fulfillment must be proven per deliverable, not inferred from capability availability. A receipt counts only when it is a local measurement or deterministic check (never a model opinion), has quality `PASS` and rights `CLEAR`, carries attribution proof when attribution is required, is at most seven days old and not in the future, matches the opportunity's offer class and the deliverable's class and capability, matches the current capability proof digest, and binds to accepted current Stage16.3 deliverable and rights evidence that reference the same artifact and are not scoped to another capability. Its portfolio entry must be a local artifact with the same identity, bytes, class and capability; one artifact cannot appear as two samples.

Delivery time is conservative: per-unit measured minutes × units × (1 + revisions) × 1.5, rounded up, plus support minutes. A sample must have measured at least the whole bundle's units including revisions for that class and capability; throughput is never extrapolated beyond an observed sample, and among contradictory covering samples the slowest one is used. Capacity must be a current measurement of the same delivery window, with existing committed work included. The resulting scenario is `ASSUMED` with `reservedCapacity: false`: the factory does not book a slot. AYAS cannot promise faster or more than it has measured; the Stage16.3B gate checks each actual order.

The revision is a SHA-256 over canonical JSON of the whole input, so property order does not change it and any commercial or evidence change does. A revision is an identity, not a signature, receipt or authority. Inputs are refused before any accessor runs; hostile proxies, sparse arrays, symbols and prototypes fail closed.

Evaluator: 59 primary +10 frozen held-out; 64 assertion-caught negative controls in an owned gitless TEMP closure. Source, digest helper, document, graders and fixture are forbidden autonomous patch targets. Live accounts, listings, money and delivery are untouched; real platform collection belongs to Stage16.4 onward.
