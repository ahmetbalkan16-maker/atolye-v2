# AYAS revenue terms and compliance boundary — 16.11A

The new pure checklist surfaces current platform terms, account standing, the exact automation use case and permission, offer rights, fees/consumer duties, jurisdiction, tax and legal review before a real pilot. It returns review requirements and refusals. It cannot approve a pilot, make legal or tax conclusions, authenticate an owner/professional/platform decision, spend, publish, connect an account or execute an operation. All outputs retain `pilotActivationAllowed=false`, `grantsAuthority=false`, `externalWrite=false`, autonomous spend 0 and no raw retention.

## Evidence and scope

`assessAyasRevenueCompliance` accepts bounded descriptor-only JSON with exact closed keys. Review receipts bind platform, account digest, offer digest, operation, terms digest and scope digest. Duplicate, restricted, malformed and cross-bound records block. The scope expires after one day; account observations after one day, public terms after 30 days and other review metadata after seven days. Future observations remain unresolved. These are conservative technical freshness ceilings, not a guarantee that terms stayed unchanged. A changed terms digest invalidates prior review bindings.

The category source vocabulary prevents a public reference or model answer from replacing official account observations or professional tax/legal review. Receipt source labels are caller metadata, not authentication. A complete synthetic checklist is `REVIEW_CHECKLIST_COMPLETE_NOT_AUTHORITY`; actual official readers, reviewed receipts and the existing owner-controlled activation path remain UNBOUND. Unknown jurisdiction/tax/legal obligations produce `PROFESSIONAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED`; other missing evidence produces `OWNER_REVIEW_REQUIRED`. AYAS does not infer jurisdiction from timezone, device location, currency or platform tax handling. No caller-supplied approval, jurisdiction interpretation, raw text, secret, customer identity, bank detail or tax identifier is accepted or retained.

Financial operations are refused according to the existing code-owned effect table. Fiverr remains manual handoff only. Automation review distinguishes manual-only work, Etsy written platform permission, Upwork's approved API use case, and references to currently reviewed official API terms for other adapters. These checklist distinctions confer no platform permission. They do not qualify Udemy/Fiverr transports that remain unbound, extend existing adapter permissions or change the production registry (EMPTY).
