# Stage16.9 — Reinvestment eligibility

The production entry point is pure advice and stays disabled: `enabled=false`, `basisPoints=0`, empty currency caps and purpose allowlist. It reads no environment, memory, file, platform or payment API. Stage16.1 and the global zero-spend policy are unchanged. No approval, reservation, executor, subscription or budget increase is installed.

`createAyasRevenueReinvestmentEvaluator` offers an unbound trusted source-policy reader for a future authenticated owner integration. Tests supply synthetic reviewed policies. A normalized policy record, digest or passing callback is not authenticated owner approval. The default export binds no reader; request/model/platform fields cannot select or enable one. A real nonzero policy still requires an owner-reviewed source configuration, authenticated binding and current ledger reconciliation.

Inputs bind the complete16.2 ledger revision and canonical digest to one currency and an exact evidence window. Numeric chronology, fee coverage and per-order payouts are checked. Gross observations, hypothetical prices, expected revenue and unsupported input fields cannot become cash. Reversals are applied globally before selecting the window. An unrelated order's payout cannot settle another sale. Fees are deducted from net sales before each order's cash cap; delivery, ad and other costs are then deducted once. This avoids treating seller payouts as gross or deducting platform fees twice.

Unknown fees, refunds, disputes, tax treatment, settlement, debt, history coverage or reserve amounts close capacity. The reconciliation is a normalized receipt with a conservative maximum age of24 hours; it is not a cryptographic bank/platform attestation. A future trusted collector must bind its provenance and complete history. Missing fee/payout rows remain unknown; explicit zero observations differ from missing facts. Refunds, taxes and prior experiment costs remain deductions. No currency conversion or cross-currency loss/revenue offset exists.

Selected-window realized profit is reduced by negative earlier realized history and capped by current lifetime settled profit, so losses after the window cannot disappear and later positive observations cannot increase the selected-window ceiling. Refund/fee/dispute/tax/payout reserves are retained. Integer `BigInt` intermediates, converted only within safe numeric bounds, compute the floored basis-point ceiling and the reviewed absolute currency cap. These use constructor syntax compatible with the project's existing ES2017 compilation target.

Free-first validation is rederived through16.3 and bound to candidate platform/currency. A separate current normalized no-equivalent-free receipt is required. Unresolved/high security, licensing or privacy risk blocks advice. The effect must be one-shot, reversible or bounded, have an exact amount/currency downside and metric, and expire within effective reconciliation validity. Recurring/subscription costs, unknown cost, credit/debt, loss chasing and automatic scaling are refused.

`OWNER_REVIEW_ELIGIBLE` carries owner approval required, execution authority NONE, spend authority false and autonomous budget0. Its immutable digest binds amount, currency, platform, purpose, window, policy, ledger, reconciliation, risks, free-first evidence, metric and expiration. It is not a reservation: overlapping proposals share no newly created balance/store. A future separately approved action must reread the ledger, reconcile existing commitments and obtain exact owner approval; it cannot execute this advisory result directly.

All proof in this packet is synthetic/source-only. No money, real owner policy, account, payment, credential, publication or production binding is tested. Stage16.5/16.7/16.8 external qualification stays deferred; Stage16/Master remain OPEN.
