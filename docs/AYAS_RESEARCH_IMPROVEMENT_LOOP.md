# AYAS Research → Improvement Learning Loop — Stage 8

## Scope and current operating state

Stage 8 connects stored external research findings to locally measured quality gaps, explicit hypotheses, disposable experiments, durable evidence and owner-reviewable proposals. It extends the existing discovery daemon, research store, proposal inbox and owner approval flow. Research content, benchmark scores and experiment verdicts have **no production mutation authority**. The experiment proposal mutation kind is deliberately absent from `AyasMutationRegistry`; approving a proposal records an owner decision but cannot execute the proposed source change.

The production registry currently contains the Stage 6 `cognitive-quality` evaluator and a closed capability map, but **zero registered change strategies**. A live finding can therefore be classified and measured; a relevant measured gap stops at `NEEDS_EXPERIMENT_DESIGN` and can enter the existing non-executable design-review proposal path. End-to-end sandbox experiments and evidence-backed proposals are proven with reviewed TEMP fixture strategies. Registering a real strategy requires a separate reviewed code change. This is not autonomous self-evolution (Stage 15).

## Existing loop and decision contract

`AyasExternalResearchStore` supplies already-recorded findings. `AyasResearchImprovementLoop` normalizes each finding into bounded, neutralized text plus structured provenance, confidence, freshness, category, license status and hashed claim identity. The classifier rejects irrelevant, unsupported, stale, weak, paid-only and instruction-bearing claims; exact and token-overlap duplicates converge. External text cannot select a benchmark, strategy, path, command, tool, model, approval decision or mutation kind.

`AyasResearchExperimentRegistry` maps a closed category to a local capability and benchmark dimensions. A claim alone never starts an experiment. A current-HEAD benchmark must show at least one failing, non-held-out case in a relevant dimension. A clean measurement with no such failure yields `NO_LOCAL_GAP`; a missing benchmark or measurement remains an explicit hold. A registered, scope-validated strategy plus that gap creates a hypothesis bound to finding IDs, base HEAD, evaluator digest, target cases, minimum target gain, protected invariants, regression suites, exact files, line budget and risk class. Paraphrased findings can share one hypothesis.

## Sandbox, measurement and verdict

Each measurement or experiment clones the exact commit into OS TEMP with `--shared`, removes the clone's remote, links the installed dependencies, and runs the benchmark as a bounded child process. The child receives mock provider settings, stripped credentials, closed proxy settings, and TEMP runtime, authority, workspace, profile and configuration paths. The runner checks the source tree and shared dependency stamp for unexpected writes. This is process and path isolation, **not an OS security sandbox**; only reviewed local evaluators and strategies may be registered.

An experiment first reruns the unchanged benchmark and any declared regression suites. Its baseline must reproduce the hypothesis's failing target set and evaluator digest. Only then does the registered strategy write its declared source files in the disposable clone. The same benchmark and suites run again. An `IMPROVED` verdict requires the minimum target gain, unchanged case set and evaluator, no newly failing case, no held-out loss, passing protected suites, bounded performance and file scope, unchanged live workspace, and successful sandbox disposal. Cleanup failure is `UNSAFE`. `NEUTRAL`, `REGRESSED`, `INVALID_EXPERIMENT`, `INCONCLUSIVE` and `UNSAFE` remain durable outcomes; negative equivalent results are not repeated indefinitely.

Evidence packages bind the finding/source identities, hypothesis, base HEAD, attempt key, evaluator, before/after scores, held-out results, regressions, bounded diff and hash, risk, environment and advisory Stage 7 route. They contain no external research body, credential or executable instruction. `AyasResearchProposalBridge` accepts only hash-verified, completed `IMPROVED` evidence at the current HEAD. It sends a proposal through ordinary discovery and owner review; the proposal neither decides nor executes. A broken evidence binding is marked stale. Internal review may reject or defer; an owner rejection suppresses that equivalent attempt.

## Restart, concurrency and PC-off behavior

The research-improvement store uses its own lock root and atomic, fsynced records. Reservations survive process death; a dead owner or expired lease becomes `UNCERTAIN` and is not promoted or replayed immediately. Retries have a cooldown and finite attempt limit. A crash after evidence write but before final checkpoint cannot create a proposal. Two processes racing against one store admit one active experiment. Each cycle classifies at most 25 findings, measures at most one gap and admits at most one experiment; at most one experiment is concurrent and three can start in 24 hours. A powered-off workstation runs nothing; restart-time backlog is processed in bounded batches. A dirty repository, stale graph or insufficient time blocks heavy work.

## Evaluation and limits

The same evaluator SHA-256 `540f4d4656d277280bbd166d8305186f64231d93a55ff8fa0eb755d5c2cc1e9a` was overlaid with its finding fixture on a clean OS-TEMP `git archive cc3e2e1` plus a dependency junction; no Stage 8 production source was overlaid. The baseline adapter uses the original disposition and proposal bridge. Baseline: 8/28 decision cases and 2/8 held-out, 23 unwarranted design proposals, seven missed experiment decisions, zero authority leaks. Final: 28/28 decision cases, 8/8 held-out, zero unwarranted proposals, missed experiments or authority leaks. The final integration gate covers 55 scenarios, including five held-out scenarios, two-process races, crash windows, injection attempts, timeout, network/credential isolation, proposal authority and the real Stage 6 evaluator on a TEMP clone. The real evaluator observed its documented 53/55 quality baseline; a neutral edit stayed neutral and a held-out regression was rejected.

Decision metrics compare guards and routing, not live model prose or actual improvement from a registered production strategy. The legacy pre-Stage-8 path already had no direct mutation authority; the baseline's 23 unwarranted proposals were advisory planning records. The existing `smoke-ayas-observer-autostart.ts` remains unsafe because its Scheduled Task fixture can unregister the real owner task, so it was not run.
