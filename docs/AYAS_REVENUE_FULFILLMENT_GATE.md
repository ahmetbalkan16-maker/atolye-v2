# AYAS fulfillment and delivery quality gate — Stage16.3B

`evaluateAyasRevenueFulfillment(input, trustedNow)` is a pure, deterministic gate over one accepted order. It follows the canonical flow order/job → requirements → plan → produce → QA → delivery manifest → owner/platform handoff → observed completion → ledger, and reports where the order stands. Every result carries `authority: NONE`, `deliversAutonomously: false`, `externalWrite: false`, `grantsSpendAuthority: false` and `createsLedgerEntry: false`. The module imports only the revenue helpers, the zero-cost policy and the Stage16.3A factory; it has no filesystem, network, credential, ledger store or executor.

**Order and promises.** The order names an offer revision. The gate re-runs the Stage16.3A factory at the order's acceptance time and requires `OWNER_REVIEW_READY` with exactly that revision, the same offer id and a mapped platform: an order cannot be sold on an offer that was not provable when it was accepted. The order may not promise more than the offer: no deliverable outside it, no more units, no more revisions, no deadline shorter than the offer's delivery window, and no out-of-scope requirement. Each of these is `BLOCKED` as `UNSUPPORTED_PROMISE`. The plan is the offer's measured delivery scenario; its work minutes drive the deadline state.

**Requirements.** Every ordered deliverable needs at least one requirement record, and every record must be `COMPLETE` with an evidence digest. Missing or ambiguous requirements give `REQUIREMENTS_INCOMPLETE`: the owner clarifies with the customer; AYAS does not message anyone.

**Production and QA.** Only files of the current revision round count. A file passes when its rights are `CLEAR` with evidence, required attribution has proof, it has at least one `PASS` receipt from a deterministic check, local measurement or owner review (never a model opinion), no `FAIL` receipt, and every counted receipt was made after that file record was produced. A deliverable needs as many passing files as ordered units. A failed file that has not been replaced gives `QA_REWORK_REQUIRED`; otherwise missing files give `IN_PRODUCTION`. Paid, subscription, metered or unknown production cost in any round, a rights-blocked file in the current round, receipts for unknown files or receipts older than their file are `BLOCKED`.

**Delivery manifest.** When every deliverable passes, the gate returns `HANDOFF_READY` with a manifest: the passing files (deliverable, SHA-256, byte size, media class) in canonical order, and a digest bound to the order id, offer revision and round. The owner performs the handoff; the gate never delivers. A recorded handoff must carry exactly that manifest digest and evidence. A handoff before requirements and QA pass, or one whose files changed afterwards, is `BLOCKED`.

**Deadline.** Before handoff the deadline is `ON_TRACK`, `AT_RISK` (files missing and less time left than the planned work) or `MISSED`. A missed deadline needs the owner (`OWNER_REVIEW_REQUIRED`); if the order is otherwise complete, the manifest is still returned, so a late handoff the owner chooses stays bound to exact files. After handoff the state is `MET_AT_HANDOFF` or `MISSED_AT_HANDOFF`; lateness is never hidden.

**Completion and ledger.** Completion must be observed from the platform or entered by the owner, with evidence, after the handoff. `ACCEPTED` gives `COMPLETED_OBSERVED` with `realizedLedgerEligible: true`. That flag means only that realized money for this order may later be recorded through the Stage16.2 ledger from its own payout evidence; the gate result is not a ledger fact and the ledger rejects it. A revision request opens the next round while the order's revision budget lasts and needs the owner after that. Disputes and cancellations always need the owner.

Inputs are bounded codes and digests; prose, PII and directive fields, accessors, proxies, sparse arrays, symbols and prototypes are refused before anything runs. Digests identify normalized receipts; the gate does not read or certify file bytes.

Evaluator: 61 primary + 10 frozen held-out; 65 assertion-caught negative controls in an owned gitless TEMP closure. Source, document, graders and fixture are forbidden autonomous patch targets. No customer, order, file transfer, platform or money is touched.
