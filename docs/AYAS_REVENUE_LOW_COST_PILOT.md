It must eventually reuse the existing authenticated owner path; no production reader, approval endpoint or new owner decision authority is bound. Unit callbacks simulate exact plan/transition review digests. READY/ACTIVE unit states explicitly retain `executionQualification=FRAMEWORK_ONLY_UNBOUND`, `executionAuthority=NONE`, owner approval required, no external write and zero autonomous spend. These states do not open any adapter write path or establish that an action was performed.

It binds the full validation snapshot, platform, account/offer digests, proposed operation, transport, deliverable/rights proof, accepted fee evidence and currency. Missing, stale, unqualified or mismatched evidence blocks. A completed checklist remains metadata, not platform permission, account authentication or legal/professional advice.

An ACTIVE window starts only in a reviewed local transition plan; its start/stop and metric stay fixed on pause/resume. A transition receipt binds the exact proposed state, window and clock. Rejection, defer, recovery-required, stale/future or mismatched review metadata stays unapproved. No automatic remote cancellation, pause, restart, publication, payment or scaling exists.
