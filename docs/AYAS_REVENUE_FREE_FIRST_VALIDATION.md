# AYAS free-first revenue validation — Stage16.3

`validateAyasRevenueFreeFirst(input, trustedNow)` is a pure, deterministic advisory evaluator. It accepts bounded descriptor-only normalized facts, snapshots and freezes them, and returns one of seven canonical statuses plus ten diagnostic dimensions. Every result carries `authority: NONE`, false action/spend flags and `realizedLedgerVerified: false`. No registry, filesystem, network, credential or executor integration exists in these three modules.

An opportunity is an explicitly hypothetical price/value/deliverable proposal. Codes replace prose; external text cannot select an operation, path or command. All monetary inputs use bounded nonnegative integer minor units and a closed currency list. No FX conversion occurs. Price and derived contribution are ASSUMED; fee/delivery inputs retain OBSERVED, ASSUMED or UNKNOWN labels and provenance. Unknown values remain null. Negative hypothetical contribution yields DO_NOT_PURSUE. Zero price has null margin. Scenario objects cannot be admitted directly as Stage16.2 ledger facts; there is no realized-ledger write binding.

A conditional PILOT_CANDIDATE requires local available capability bound to current proof, a proven deliverable, current non-conflicting rights proof, zero-cost prerequisites bound to suitable current proof, known fee scenarios based on current platform fee evidence, nonnegative scenario contribution, competition/differentiation evidence, and two independent current demand provenance groups. This is source advice for subsequent offer design; publish/submit/message/account/financial actions still pass their separate owner and spend gates.

Demand groups join whenever publisher identity OR original-reference digest is shared, including transitive mirror chains. Repeated evidence cannot corroborate itself. A current negative demand fact invalidates its provenance group's positive signal. Current missing/degraded capability, unproven deliverable, uncertain rights and contradictory competition/differentiation facts conservatively defeat the corresponding positive claims. Model opinions, owner enthusiasm, listing counts and unverified realized-ledger signals cannot establish market demand.

Opportunity/evidence/platform/market scope must match; shared MULTI local capability/deliverable proof is allowed only for the same opportunity and market. Both platform and offer-type freshness ceilings are code owned: offer DIGITAL_PRODUCT30/FREELANCE_SERVICE7/COURSE60/CONTENT_ASSET14/OTHER7 days; platform Etsy30/Upwork7/Fiverr14/Udemy60/LemonSqueezy30/MULTI7. Effective freshness is the minimum of those ceilings and the record's declared expiry. These are conservative internal defaults, not externally certified platform policy. Future or stale evidence is excluded. Paid, subscription, metered and unknown evidence/prerequisite cost is refused.

Input digests identify normalized evidence; this module does not authenticate publishers or externally certify fee accuracy, licensing or market truth. Fee assumptions are eligible only with current platform fee-schedule basis, remain ASSUMED, and produce hypothetical economics. Multi-platform or owner-required account readiness yields owner-review advice. Actual evidence collection and platform/account integration belong to subsequent canonical stages.

Evaluator: 66 primary +12 frozen held-out; 39 assertion-caught negative controls in an owned gitless TEMP closure. Held-out H05's initial expected status was corrected for the pre-existing unknown-fee priority when both fee and market scope are absent; the scenario itself is unchanged. P64's assertion was corrected to the existing classifier API's `level`. Original pre-repair receipt is retained separately and is not final validation. Evaluated rights dimensions show UNPROVEN/UNCERTAIN when a raw CLEAR claim lacks valid proof. Source/docs/graders/fixture are forbidden autonomous patch targets.

Canonical completion also requires affected regressions, TypeScript/lint/diff, independent review, local source commit, current Graphify and clean exact-source evidence. The last complete baseline remains bound to its recorded source HEAD; selected checks do not claim a new full-system baseline. Live revenue collection/activation remains unperformed. Local commits only; NO PUSH under the latest master continuation.
